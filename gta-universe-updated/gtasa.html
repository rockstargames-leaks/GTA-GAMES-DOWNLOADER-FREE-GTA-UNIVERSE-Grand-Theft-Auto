Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

About GTA San Andreas

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas was released in 2004 and follows Carl "CJ" Johnson as he returns to Los Santos after the death of his mother. The game features three major cities, a huge countryside, dozens of vehicles, aircraft, gang wars, character customization, and one of the largest open worlds of its time.

Game Information

Developer Rockstar Games
Release Year 2004
Main Character Carl "CJ" Johnson
Map Los Santos, San Fierro, Las Venturas
Genre Open World Action Adventure
