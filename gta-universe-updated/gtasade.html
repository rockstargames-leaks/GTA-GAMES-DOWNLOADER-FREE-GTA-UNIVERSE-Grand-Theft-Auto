GTA San Andreas - Definitive Edition

About

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas – Definitive Edition is the remastered version of the legendary 2004 classic. The game follows Carl "CJ" Johnson as he returns to Los Santos to save his family and rebuild Grove Street. The Definitive Edition introduces modern graphics, improved controls, upgraded lighting, enhanced textures, and numerous gameplay improvements while keeping the original story and world intact.

Game Information

Developer Rockstar Games / Grove Street Games
Publisher Rockstar Games

Game Engine Unreal Engine
Genre Open World Action Adventure
