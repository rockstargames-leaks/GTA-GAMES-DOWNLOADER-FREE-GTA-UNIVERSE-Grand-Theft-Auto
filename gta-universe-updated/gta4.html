Grand Theft Auto IV

About GTA IV

Grand Theft Auto IV was released in 2008 and follows Niko Bellic, an Eastern European war veteran who travels to Liberty City in search of a better life. The game introduced realistic physics, improved AI, a detailed open world, and a mature story.

Game Information

Developer Rockstar Games
Publisher Rockstar Games
Release Year 2008
Main Character Niko Bellic
Location Liberty City
Genre Open World Action Adventure

Main Features

  • Massive Liberty City
  • Realistic Euphoria physics engine
  • Story-driven missions
  • Online multiplayer
  • Helicopters and motorcycles
  • Improved driving mechanics
  • Interactive mobile phone
  • Taxi, police and emergency missions
