GTA Vice City - Definitive Edition

About

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City – Definitive Edition brings Tommy Vercetti's classic adventure back with improved graphics, modern controls, enhanced lighting, higher-resolution textures, longer draw distances, and gameplay improvements while keeping the iconic 1980s atmosphere intact.

Game Information

Developer Rockstar Games / Grove Street Games
Publisher Rockstar Games
Original Release 2002
Definitive Edition 2021 (Part of GTA Trilogy)
Steam Release January 19, 2023
Main Character Tommy Vercetti
Location Vice City
Engine Unreal Engine

Features

  • HD textures
  • Modern GTA V-style controls
  • Improved weapon wheel
  • Enhanced lighting and shadows
  • Higher resolution environments
  • Improved checkpoints
  • Performance improvements
  • Achievements support
