About Grand Theft Auto 2

Grand Theft Auto 2, released in 1999, is the second main title in the Grand Theft Auto series. Set in the futuristic Anywhere City, players complete missions for rival gangs while earning money, increasing respect, and surviving intense police chases.

Story

Players take control of Claude Speed, a criminal trying to become the most powerful figure in Anywhere City. By working for different gangs, earning respect, and completing dangerous missions, players unlock new districts while avoiding the police and rival organizations.

Gameplay Highlights

Grand Theft Auto 2 expanded on the original game by introducing a gang reputation system, allowing players to earn or lose respect with different criminal organizations. The game also featured improved graphics, more vehicles, a larger city, better sound effects, and more varied missions, making it one of the most influential top-down action games of its era.
