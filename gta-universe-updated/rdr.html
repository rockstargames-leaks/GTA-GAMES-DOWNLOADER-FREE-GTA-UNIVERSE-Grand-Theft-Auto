Red Dead Redemption

About the Game

Red Dead Redemption is an award-winning open-world western action-adventure game developed by Rockstar San Diego and published by Rockstar Games. Players take the role of former outlaw John Marston as he travels across the American frontier hunting down members of his former gang.

Story

After being forced into service by government agents, John Marston begins a dangerous journey across New Austin, Mexico and West Elizabeth to capture his old gang members. His adventure is filled with memorable characters, intense gunfights and emotional storytelling.
