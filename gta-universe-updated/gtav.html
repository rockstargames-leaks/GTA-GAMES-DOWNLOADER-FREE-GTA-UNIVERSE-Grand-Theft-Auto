Grand Theft Auto V

About GTA V

Grand Theft Auto V was released in 2013 and is set in the vast state of San Andreas. Players experience the interconnected stories of Michael De Santa, Franklin Clinton, and Trevor Philips while completing missions across Los Santos and Blaine County.

Game Information

Developer Rockstar North
Publisher Rockstar Games
Release Year 2013
Main Characters Michael, Franklin & Trevor
Location Los Santos & Blaine County
Genre Open World Action Adventure

Main Features

  • Huge open-world map
  • Three playable protagonists
  • Dynamic mission switching
  • Story Mode and GTA Online
  • Hundreds of vehicles
  • Aircraft and boats
  • Heists
  • Character customization
  • Businesses and properties
  • Rockstar Editor (PC)
