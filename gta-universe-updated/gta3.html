Grand Theft Auto III

About GTA III

Grand Theft Auto III is an open-world action-adventure game released in 2001. Set in Liberty City, players control Claude as he rises through the criminal underworld. GTA III introduced a fully 3D open world and changed the gaming industry.

Game Information

Developer Rockstar Games
Release Year 2001
Setting Liberty City
Main Character Claude
Genre Open World Action

Features

  • Story missions
  • Classic radio stations
  • Many vehicles
