Grand Theft Auto: Vice City

About the Game

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City was released in 2002 and is set in the vibrant, 1980s-inspired Vice City. Players follow Tommy Vercetti as he builds a criminal empire through action-packed missions, memorable characters, and a legendary soundtrack.

Game Information

Developer Rockstar Games
Release Year 2002
Main Character Tommy Vercetti
Location Vice City
Genre Open World Action-Adventure

Key Features

  • 1980s Miami-inspired open world
  • Iconic soundtrack and radio stations
  • Over 100 vehicles
  • Motorcycles, boats and helicopters
  • Property purchasing system
  • Story missions and side missions
  • Classic cheat codes
