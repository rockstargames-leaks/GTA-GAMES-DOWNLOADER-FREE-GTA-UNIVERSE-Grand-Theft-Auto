Red Dead Redemption 2

The Epic Western Adventure

About the Game

Red Dead Redemption 2 is an open-world western action-adventure game developed by Rockstar Games. Set in 1899, the game follows Arthur Morgan, a trusted member of the Van der Linde gang, as the gang struggles to survive during the final days of the American frontier.

Story

Following a failed robbery in the town of Blackwater, the Van der Linde gang is forced to flee across America. As lawmen and bounty hunters close in, Arthur Morgan must choose between loyalty to the gang and doing what he believes is right. Every decision shapes his journey through one of the most detailed open worlds ever created.

Wildlife

The world of Red Dead Redemption 2 contains more than 200 species of animals, including bears, wolves, deer, cougars, alligators, birds, fish and many other creatures. Hunting and fishing are major gameplay activities and provide resources for crafting, trading and survival.
