Grand Theft Auto

The Game That Started It All

About Grand Theft Auto

Grand Theft Auto, released in 1997, is the first game in the legendary Grand Theft Auto series. Players complete missions across three cities while driving vehicles, earning money, and avoiding the police in a top-down, open-world action game.

Story

Players begin as a criminal working for various gangs in Liberty City, San Andreas and Vice City. By completing missions and increasing their criminal reputation, they rise through the ranks of the underworld while evading law enforcement.

Gameplay Highlights

Grand Theft Auto introduced the open-world crime formula that later became one of the most successful video game franchises in history. Players can freely explore cities, steal vehicles, complete missions, evade police, earn money, and unlock new areas as they progress through the game.
