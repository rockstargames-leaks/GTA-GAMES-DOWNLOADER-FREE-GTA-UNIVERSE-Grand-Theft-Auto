About GTA Universe

The Ultimate GTA Fan Website

Who We Are

GTA Universe is an unofficial fan-made website created for Grand Theft Auto players and enthusiasts. Our goal is to provide game information, guides, cheat codes, screenshots, trailers, and the latest news about the GTA series in one place.

Our Mission

We aim to build a friendly community where GTA fans can discover information about classic and modern Grand Theft Auto games, follow official Rockstar announcements, and enjoy helpful resources.
